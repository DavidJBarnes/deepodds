## Part 2 — the crown jewel: Kalshi crypto tails are overpriced vs Deribit

Deribit is the sharp crypto-vol venue; its risk-neutral P(BTC>=K) is the best free
estimate of the true tail prob. **Live snapshot (BTC ~$62k, n=27 KXBTCD upper tails in
the 1-12c band):**
- mean Kalshi-implied **2.87c** vs mean Deribit-fair **0.79c**
- Kalshi richer on **100%** of tails, mean gap **+2.08c** (median +1.5, max +5.3)
- **EV of selling at Kalshi bid vs Deribit-fair = +1.18c/contract**, and Kalshi **fee ~0**
  at tail prices (the fee-curve point from idea #2)

So Kalshi retail pays ~2.6x the sharp-market fair for "BTC moons today," at ~zero fee.
This is "rent the sharper venue's price" applied to our surviving archetype — NOT
forecasting. **Caveats:** snapshot only (one moment/spot); N(d2) with nearest Deribit
expiry/strike is approximate; **upper-tail-only = a directional short-BTC-upside book**,
not delta-neutral -> per-underlying correlation cap is mandatory. Must be forward-
validated through settlement (that's what the oracle daemon does).
